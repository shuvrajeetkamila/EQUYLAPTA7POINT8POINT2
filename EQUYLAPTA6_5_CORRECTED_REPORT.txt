 17. What Was Actually Demonstrated
1. Token-level next-token prediction agreement (17.50%) is an incomplete measure of functional equivalence, as behavioral choice agreement is substantially higher (25.60%).
2. Layer 0 representations can be linearly aligned across dimensions (Linear CKA = 0.4631).
3. Shallow transfer produces positive task delta at 2L (+8.80 pp) and Architecture C (+5.60 pp).
4. The translated intervention exerts causal influence on model execution (reversibly altering accuracy).
5. In deeper networks, downstream layers (1..D-1) dilute single-layer interventions, leading to depth-dependent instability.

 18. What Was NOT Demonstrated
1. Successful cross-architecture functional transfer at canonical depth 4L (translated model underperformed target-local control and random controls).
2. Convergence of independent target reconstructions (agreement was 38.33%, converged = false).
3. Restoration of beneficial function (intervention degraded math performance relative to baseline).
4. Monotonic depth degradation law (deltas sequence is non-monotonic).
5. That the 13–17% ceiling is merely an evaluation artifact.

2. Can multi-layer joint translation prevent downstream representational drift in deep networks?
3. Would non-linear representation projectors bridge dimensional gaps without inducing negative transfer?
