29. WHAT ACTUALLY APPEARS TO TRANSFER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Evidence-based answer, factor by factor:
   1. A SITE-LEVEL INTERVENTION BENEFIT: inserting small/organized
      changes at the layer-0 attention region lifts 3-seed math mean
      even with RANDOM values (D +1.67) — but randomly.
   2. THE REAL ORGANIZED WEIGHTS add a small marginal advantage
      (A-D = +0.84,
      within seed noise) AND, more importantly, a qualitatively
      cleaner capability profile (section 19 vector lens).
   3. SOURCE-BASIS ALIGNMENT is required (H collapses to 15.0).
   4. The 150-step surrogate reproduces the GAIN (25.0) WITHOUT any
      source weight — but destroys the 'functional transfer'
      interpretation, because it does NOT reproduce the circuit's
      function (13.3% agreement).  What transfers reproducibly is the
      intervention effect at that site, not a model-independent
      functional component.
   5. Destroy/restore (J 14.17 -> K 19.17, exact) proves the trained
      surrogate's own arrangement causally matters — the sequence is
      real, but it is evidence about the SURROGATE's function (a
      target-adapted mild regularizer), not the source circuit's.

30. WHAT DOES NOT APPEAR TO TRANSFER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * The FUNCTION of the circuit: surrogate agreement 13.3%; source
     with surrogate inserted scores 32.5 vs 60.0 with the real circuit.
     L8 (functional reconstruction) NOT achieved.
   * Topology alone: F (sites+shapes, matched random values) = 17.5,
     BELOW baseline — the structural footprint has no positive effect.
   * Weight statistics alone: G (values permuted) = 20.83 ~= baseline.
   * A special minimal core: real subsets never beat matched random
     subsets at any fraction (10% held-out: real 22.5 vs random
     [22.5, 25.0, 25.0]).
   * Capability-specific improvement: at the vector seed the A
     intervention LOWERS math (-2.5) while raising lang (+15) —
     mixed, non-specific; consistent with E4's interference finding.
   * More surrogate training helps: 600 steps (19.17) is WORSE than
     150 steps (25.0) — overfitting the train corpus destroys the
     transfer benefit; the effect lives at mild-intervention scale.

31. REMAINING UNCERTAINTY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * 3 held-out seeds per cell: A-D separation (+0.84) is inside seed
     noise (A std 3.12); the marginal weight contribution could be zero.
   * The B-L2 exception (23.33 at layer 2) shows a second working
     region; 'location matters' is really 'placement region matters'.
   * Minimality used calibration subsets of 5 members — resolution is
     coarse (fractions move one member at a time).
   * Equivalence measured on the source only; a target-side reference
     function does not exist by construction.
   * One model pair, one depth, one capability; the surrogate's
     'benign intervention' reading needs replication on other pairs.
   STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION for the original E4 result: a
   mild layer-0 intervention acts as a regularizer/noise-injection
   that slightly helps this target's math suite; the circuit's
   organization adds at most a small cleanliness advantage — not a
   transferred capability.
